Provenance and hash pinning for the binaries under tools/butler/<rid>/:
four independent checks against the pinned SHA-256 values recorded in the fetching notes of the
Puck build repo (itch.io publishes no checksum file). The authoritative pin list for the launcher
is ButlerSidecar.Pins in this repository; deploy.verify-butler-pins.py checks a release against it.
